# Support Policy

## Included
- self-service documentation
- built-in diagnostics
- automated recovery for known safe failures
- product defect reporting
- 30 days of installation/activation assistance

## Not included
- customer project debugging
- coding-agent consulting
- bespoke integrations
- custom deployment work
- 24x7 support
- unlimited human troubleshooting

Support scope may evolve as higher-value team/enterprise products are introduced.
