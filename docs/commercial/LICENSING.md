# Licensing — Working Commercial Policy

Initial model hypothesis:
- perpetual local commercial-use license
- unlimited repositories/projects
- limited device activations
- no redistribution, sublicensing, or resale of EF binaries
- customer retains ownership of customer code/data
- EF retains ownership of EF software, original workflows, UI, documentation, presets and platform IP
- third-party components remain subject to their own licenses

Purchase includes 12 months of update eligibility. Pricing and device counts remain hypotheses until validated.
